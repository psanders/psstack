# Hook library

The hook is the only line guaranteed to be read (the line before LinkedIn's "…see more"
fold; tweet 1 on X). **Every pattern below is drawn from Pedro's real posts** (researched
2026-06-29; impressions are trailing-365-day). Pick one that matches the pillar, then make
it concrete with the prompt's detail.

Rules of thumb:
- One idea per hook. No sub-clauses.
- Put a number, a named entity, or a tension in it.
- Italic-Unicode the named entity once (`𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐`, `𝘍𝘰𝘯𝘰𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘳`, `𝘘𝘊𝘰𝘣𝘳𝘰`, `𝙆𝘼𝙍𝙈𝘼`).
- **No em-dash.** End on the noun that matters.
- A `↓` / `⤵` on its own line right after the hook is Pedro's standard "read on" beat.

## Industry Take

- **industry-frustration** — *"The Voice AI industry is in desperate need of 𝙆𝘼𝙍𝙈𝘼."* (6,285 impr / 39 comments, the top template). Names a gap and an answer.
- **contrarian-flex** — *"𝘍𝘰𝘯𝘰𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘳 is the smallest 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 company in the planet (by far.)"* (6,209). Self-deprecating flex that's actually a positioning claim.
- **contrarian-reframe** — *"Nobody knows what 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 is. (And that's a good thing.)"* (5,088). State a problem, then flip it to opportunity in the parenthetical.
- **stat-shock** — *"99% of phone systems are stuck in 1999."* A surprising number as the whole hook.
- **contrarian-prediction** — *"Is speech-to-speech the future of 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐? I'm not so sure. Not yet."* / *"Who will win the 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 race? (It's not who you think.)"*
- **myth-bust** — *"The biggest pain in 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 is not what everyone thinks."* / *"𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 is being pitched for every industry on earth. Most of those bets won't pay back."*
- **reframe-the-future** — *"The future of AI isn't smarter models. It's encoding YOUR process into commands YOU can repeat."*
- **gap-callout** — *"Most 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 platforms have shockingly thin SIP support."*
- **confession-contrarian** — *"I spent more time than I admit duct taping 𝙆𝘼𝙍𝙈𝘼 in 2025. And I won't do it again."* Earns the contrarian take by admitting you lived it.
- **slow-reveal** — *"The more I look at it, the more obvious it gets:"* (then the take).

## Operator's Journal

- **pivot-announcement** — *"New adventure. 🚀 I've started a new company:"* (8,817 impr, his single highest-reach post).
- **true-story-dialogue** — *"True story. Someone asked me the other day: 'How much would you sell 𝘍𝘰𝘯𝘰𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘳 for?'"* (7,971). Open on a real exchange; let it build to a line.
- **operator-on-the-road** — *"I've been on the road. Preaching 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 across the DR 🇩🇴. ⤵"* (5,403).
- **confession** — *"Confession: I suck at sales. So I retreat into building."* Name the flaw, reframe it as a system to debug.
- **question-confession** — *"What do you do when failure is not an option?"* / *"Last night I sat down with a question I keep avoiding:"*
- **contrarian-choice** — *"Most founders chase the biggest market they can imagine. I went the other way."*
- **nostalgia / full-circle** — *"Full circle moment 🌀"*

## Educational Tactical

- **comparison** — *"Someone asked me the other day: What's the difference between 𝘍𝘰𝘯𝘰𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘳 and Vapi, Bland, Retell, etc.?"* (7,536 / 19 comments). Position by contrast.
- **short-imperative** — *"VoIP engineer? Learn how to integrate 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 with legacy systems. Make the money bag. 💰💰💰"* (3,752 / 104 reactions in **three lines** — proof a tight hook beats length).
- **complexity-to-list** — *"𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 has so many moving pieces. Get one wrong, and the whole thing falls apart. ⤵"* (then a `⟡` layer list; carousel).
- **list-roadmap** — *"Four open-source tools for VoIP troubleshooting."* / *"How to start in 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 as a voice engineer; 4 pillars. 6 steps."*
- **prediction-explainer** — *"𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 will feel like AGI when we learn to measure End of Thought (EoT)."* (27 comments — a teaching hook that invites debate).

## Cross-pillar (use sparingly)

- **demand-validation** — *"Raise your hand if [training/product] would help. I'm gauging demand."* Once in a while; overuse reads as needy.
- **giveaway** — *"I'm giving away my 𝙍𝙤𝙪𝙩𝙧 𝙃𝙖𝙣𝙙𝙗𝙤𝙤𝙠. Drop 'Handbook' in the comments."* (61 comments — comment-to-get drives reach, but use rarely).

## Anti-patterns (never open with these)

- "I'm excited to announce…" — buries the news, signals press release.
- "What do you think?" as the *hook* — that's a weak close, not an opener.
- "Here's the truth…" / "The reality is…" / "Most people get this wrong" with no specific payoff — empty influencer tells.
- A question the reader can't answer without reading your mind.
- **Two ideas joined by an em-dash.** Split them; drop the em-dash. (Several of Pedro's older posts did this — e.g. "an Asterisk pro—but never touched ARI". Don't going forward.)
- A hook longer than ~12 words on X, or one that wraps past the LinkedIn fold.
- Hashtags in the hook.

When the prompt doesn't imply a hook, scan this list for the pillar, pick the pattern that
fits the prompt's center of gravity, and make it concrete with the prompt's actual detail.
