## The four pillars in detail

### 1. Build-in-Public — Fonoster, Routr, QCobro & Voice AI infra
- **What it is:** shipped features, milestones, performance numbers, GitHub moments, demo videos, "we just fixed X."
- **Buyer:** developers, voice AI builders, CTOs evaluating infra.
- **Center of gravity:** *what we shipped.*
- **Real hooks that worked:** "𝘍𝘰𝘯𝘰𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘳 made it to the GitHub Trending. 🚀" (4,160 impr), "My open-source project has quietly made over half a million dollars", "I made a bet on 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 and picked the least sexy vertical I could find" (QCobro v0.1.0), "Today I did something wild. 🤯".
- **Default CTA:** GitHub repo, Discord (dub.sh/6pi6TNf), or qcobro.com.
- **Tells of a good one:** a concrete number, a named component, something the reader could go try today.

### 2. Industry Take — opinions about Voice AI, telecom, open-source voice
- **What it is:** hot takes, contrarian predictions, "what nobody says out loud", infra-vs-model arguments, hype-vs-reality.
- **Buyer:** operators, founders, technical buyers.
- **Center of gravity:** *what I think.*
- **Real hooks that worked:** "The Voice AI industry is in desperate need of 𝙆𝘼𝙍𝙈𝘼." (6,285 impr / 39 comments — the top template), "𝘍𝘰𝘯𝘰𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘳 is the smallest 𝘝𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦 𝘈𝘐 company in the planet (by far.)" ## Buyer cluster archetypes

Picture these when you write (especially Operator's Journal — the post should be plausible to them):

- **Open-source telephony engineers** — the Kamailio / Asterisk / RTPEngine / Homer / ARI world. They smell hand-wavy claims instantly. Win them with specifics.
- **Voice AI builders** wiring STT/LLM/TTS into real call flows. They care about latency, EoT, barge-in, the bidirectional stream (Pedro: "the stream is 70% of the work").
- **CCaaS / contact-center leaders** evaluating voice agents against production constraints.
- **Founders / CTOs** deciding build-vs-buy on voice infra; the audience for "Fonoster vs Vapi/Bland/Retell."
- **LATAM / Dominican finance & microfinance lenders** — the QCobro buyer. Spanish-first, compliance-bound, recovery-rate-driven. Deals "close in person, coffee in hand."

If a draft would read as generic to all of these, it has drifted. Rewrite until only Pedro could have written it.

## Weekly cadence (2 Build / 2 Industry / 2 Operator / 1 Educational)

LinkedIn, daily. Each day maps to a pillar; the pillar sets hook + CTA.

| Day | Pillar | Default CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Industry Take | Engagement question (or pedrosanders.me) |
| Tue | Build-in-Public | GitHub / Discord |
| Wed | Operator's Journal | DM-me / advisory |
| Thu | Educational Tactical | KARMA waitlist |
| Fri | Build-in-Public | Repo / qcobro.com |
| Sat | Industry Take | Engagement question (or pedrosanders.me) |
| Sun | Operator's Journal | Engagement question (or no close) |

For a **week of posts**: 7 drafts in this mix, vary hook patterns (no repeat in one week), and make sure the week contains (a) the weekly paid-offer mention (Fri or Sat) and (b) the weekly lead-magnet post (Thu).

### Optional: Flexing Wednesday

On a week with a genuine milestone, Wednesday's Operator's Journal slot can flex into a
Build-in-Public hybrid — a story-shaped flex about a shipped thing. Use sparingly; only
when there's a real win.

## The four monetization-aware rules

1. **Mention a paid offer at least once a week** (Fri or Sat names it in the CTA). Pedro's historical gap: he mentions paid offers rarely.
2. **One post per week is a hyper-specific lead magnet** (Thu Educational Tactical — a checklist/framework/comparison that's save-worthy alone).
3. **Every other post carries one instantly applicable tip.**
4. **Every post connects to the niche** — Voice AI infra + the operators who build it (+ the LATAM collections vertical).
